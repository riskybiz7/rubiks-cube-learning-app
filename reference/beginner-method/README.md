# Beginner method: stage photos

Drop photos of the cube **at the end of each stage** here, the way you teach it.

## How to shoot

- Two shots per stage if possible:
  - `-top`: looking straight down at the top face
  - `-angle`: tilted so the top and two side faces are visible
- Hold the cube the way you'd hold it at that stage.
- Name files in order: `01-daisy-top.jpg`, `01-daisy-angle.jpg`, `02-white-cross-top.jpg`, …
  (any image format is fine.)

Beginner guides differ most in stages 5 to 8 (order of orienting vs. positioning
the last layer), which is why your photos matter.
